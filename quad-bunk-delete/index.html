WHY DELETE A BUNK?

THE SMARTEST SWAP IN FAMILY CARAVANS.

Most families of four or five never use the fourth bunk. It becomes the dumping ground for bags, toys and washing. This layout turns it into storage that earns its keep.

The quad bunk delete keeps three proper full-length bunks for the kids and swaps the fourth for a full-height storage zone, with shelving, hanging space and room for everything that usually ends up on the floor.

Everyone still gets their own bed. The van just stops feeling full the moment you pack for the school holidays.
